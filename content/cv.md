## Current roles

* **CheckSig.** Co-founder and CEO. Bitcoin and crypto solutions for private and institutional investors.
* **Digital Gold Institute.** Founder and Scientific Director. R&D, consultancy and training about bitcoin and blockchain technology.
* **Crypto Asset Lab**, Department of Business and Law, University of Milano-Bicocca. Founder and Scientific Director. Chairs the CAL conference.
* **ESSEC Business School.** Adjunct professor.

## Earlier career

Ferdinando worked in finance from 1996 to 2018, more than twenty years. He discovered bitcoin in 2014. From then on it took a growing share of his work. He left the bank in 2018 and stopped teaching finance in 2022.

* **2016 to 2018, Banca IMI (Intesa Sanpaolo Group).** Proof of concept of cash digitization on a private bitcoin-protocol blockchain.
* **2016, Intesa Sanpaolo, Chief Innovation Office.** Head of Blockchain and Virtual Currencies.
* **2007 to 2015, Banca IMI, Financial Engineering.** Head of Rate Curves and Clearing Tools.
* **2006 to 2007, Banca Caboto, Financial Engineering.** Head of Front Office Quantitative R&D.
* **2004 to 2005, Monte Paschi Asset Management.** Head of special projects, reporting to the CEO.
* **2002 to 2003, Monte Paschi Asset Management.** Risk Management Office: valuation of derivative and structured products.
* **2000 to 2002, RiskMap.** Co-founder and managing partner. RiskMap is now part of Confluence Technologies.
* **1999 to 2000, Banca Caboto.** Interest rate junior trader.
* **1996 to 1999, Banca Caboto.** Front office quantitative analyst, then head of the fixed-income team.
