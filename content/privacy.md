---
title: "Privacy"
description: "What this site does with visit data."
---
This site sets no cookies. It stores nothing in your browser except your choice of light or dark theme.

* **Hosting:** the site is served by GitHub Pages and Cloudflare (CDN). Both process visit data such as IP addresses to deliver pages.
* **Statistics:** Cloudflare Web Analytics and Metricool count visits. Neither uses cookies or local storage. Cloudflare measures page load times and does not track individual visitors. Metricool receives the page address, the referrer, the window size and your IP address.
* **Contact form:** the form is a Google Form. What you write there is processed by Google and read by me.

For privacy requests, write through the [contact form](https://bit.ly/contactametrano).
