Interest Rate Derivatives was taught at the Department of Statistics and Quantitative Methods, University of Milano-Bicocca, from Fall 2013 to the 2021-22 edition.

The course has a practitioner approach: it presents current market best practices, often not yet documented in textbooks, and covers the valuation of linear, plain vanilla and exotic derivatives, with rate and credit curve construction and collateralization.

* [October-December 2020](/ird202010/)
