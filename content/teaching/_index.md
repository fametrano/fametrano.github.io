I teach [Bitcoin and Blockchain Technology](/bbt/) as adjunct professor at the University of Milano-Bicocca, the University of Milan (La Statale) and ESSEC Business School. Earlier I taught [Interest Rate Derivatives](/ird/) at Milano-Bicocca. Each edition has its own page with slides, code and videos.

These courses are for the students of those universities. Anyone else, please consider the [DGI workshop](https://dgi.io/workshop).
