---
title: "Privacy"
description: "What this site does with visit data."
---
This site does not use cookies. It stores nothing in your browser except your choice of light or dark theme.

* **Hosting:** the site is served by GitHub Pages and Cloudflare (CDN). Both process visit data such as IP addresses to deliver pages.
* **Statistics:** Cloudflare Web Analytics and Metricool count visits. Neither uses cookies or local storage. Both receive the page address, the referrer, the browser type and your IP address. Metricool also receives the window size. Cloudflare measures page load times. Cloudflare does not track individual visitors.
* **Contact form:** the form is a Google Form. What you write there is processed by Google and read by me.

For privacy requests, write through the [contact form](https://bit.ly/contactametrano).
