---
title: "Privacy"
description: "What this site does with visit data."
---
This site sets no cookies. Its own code stores only your choice of light or dark theme in your browser.

* **Hosting:** the site is served by GitHub Pages and Cloudflare (CDN). Both process visit data such as IP addresses to deliver pages.
* **Statistics:** Cloudflare Web Analytics and Metricool count visits. Neither uses cookies or local storage. Both receive the page address, the referrer, the browser and operating system, with versions, and your IP address. Metricool also receives the window size. Cloudflare measures page load times. Cloudflare does not track individual visitors.
* **Video:** one post embeds two YouTube videos in privacy-enhanced mode. The YouTube player sets no cookies. It stores data in your browser when the page loads. Playing a video is handled by YouTube.
* **Contact form:** the form is a Google Form. What you write there is processed by Google and read by me.

For privacy requests, write through the [contact form](https://bit.ly/contactametrano).
